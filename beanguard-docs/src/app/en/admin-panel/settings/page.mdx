# Settings

The **Settings** tab groups your company's legal and billing details — used on pro-forma invoices, licence certificates, and in transactional emails. {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Company details {{ id: 'firma' }}

Company name, tax ID (NIP), KRS, phone, and address — the seller details that appear on the [licence certificate](/en/admin-panel/pdf-templates) and pro-forma invoice, and in the footer of transactional emails. The **Bank account number** is used only on the pro-forma invoice, as payment details.

<Note>
  The **Company registry number (KRS)** field is optional — an empty value
  simply omits that row on the pro-forma invoice.
</Note>

## Sales & payments {{ id: 'sprzedaz' }}

- **Currency** — the currency code (e.g. `PLN`) used in the shop to display prices, on the pro-forma invoice, and in the calculations in the order confirmation email.
- **VAT rate (%)** — used when converting net/gross prices in the shop, on the pro-forma invoice, and in the order confirmation email.
- **Pro forma payment term (days)** — the number of days from issuing the pro-forma invoice to the payment due date shown on the document.

## Legal documents {{ id: 'dokumenty' }}

**Terms of service URL** and **Privacy policy URL** — the addresses of your legal documents, exposed to the [shop](/en/shop) via the public configuration endpoint (`/api/open/config`), so it can link them, e.g. in the footer or the order form.

## Licence certificate {{ id: 'certyfikat' }}

**Licence activation instructions on certificate** — any text inserted into the [PDF certificate template](/en/admin-panel/pdf-templates) as the `{{ activationText }}` placeholder (use double curly braces in the template, like the other placeholders) — typically a short instruction on where in your application to paste the received licence key.
